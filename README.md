# PathBloom

### Discover Skills. Explore Careers. Build Your Future.

PathBloom is a career guidance platform designed to help students make informed academic and career decisions through structured pathways, scholarship information, college exploration, and personalized career recommendations.

The platform is focused on supporting students who may have limited access to career counseling, helping them understand educational and career opportunities available after 10th and 12th grade through an accessible and student-friendly experience.

---

## Overview

Many students face challenges when deciding:

* Which stream to choose after 10th standard
* Which courses are available after 12th standard
* What scholarships they are eligible for
* Which colleges fit their budget and interests
* What career opportunities exist in different domains
* What skills are required to achieve their goals

PathBloom addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform where students can explore educational pathways, discover careers, and learn about scholarships and institutions relevant to their aspirations.

---

## Key Features

### Career Explorer

Explore multiple career domains, including:

* Engineering
* Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
* Cybersecurity
* Commerce
* Medical Sciences
* Design
* Government Services
* Agriculture
* Arts & Humanities
* Entrepreneurship

Each career path includes:

* Career overview
* Required skills
* Educational roadmap
* Salary insights
* Future opportunities

### After 10th Guidance

Students can explore pathways such as:

* Science Stream
* Commerce Stream
* Arts & Humanities
* Diploma Programs
* Polytechnic Education
* ITI Programs
* Skill-Based Learning Paths

### After 12th Guidance

Provides guidance on:

* Engineering Programs
* Medical Courses
* Commerce and Finance Programs
* Law
* Design
* Government Examinations
* Professional Certifications
* Vocational Courses

### College Explorer

Browse colleges with information such as:

* Location
* Courses Offered
* Approximate Fee Structure
* Hostel Availability
* Popular Departments
* Placement Information
* Scholarship Support

### Scholarship Hub

Access information about:

* Government Scholarships
* Merit Scholarships
* First Graduate Benefits
* Girls Education Schemes
* Low-Income Student Support Programs
* Higher Education Assistance Initiatives

Students can review eligibility criteria, benefits, required documents, and application resources.

### Career Assessment Quiz

An interactive assessment designed to help students identify:

* Suitable educational streams
* Potential career domains
* Skill development recommendations
* Learning pathways aligned with their interests
